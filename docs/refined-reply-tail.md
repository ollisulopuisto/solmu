# Reply tails

A message replying to another has one attached chain-tail. Click it, or focus
it and press Enter, to jump to that exact original. The existing connecting
pipe appears on pointer hover or keyboard focus. Every real reply keeps its
own target, including multiple replies to one original and album members.

The former internal enter-sign chip and separate Original button are gone.
The loaded-reply footer is unchanged. Existing overlap and ordinary tails are
unchanged. A reply tail does not imply that its original is already loaded;
the existing source-loading and jump-back behavior still applies.

Verification: exact helper pointer/keyboard and geometry tests passed locally.
Full app tests and actual native dark/light captures are required before
readiness. No runtime Beeper payload or installed Mac acceptance is claimed.
